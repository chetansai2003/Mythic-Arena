The version-1 catalog has no separate ultimate command or legendary card definitions. The stronger summon/impact presentation is a visual treatment of existing legal actions; no rules, rarity, immutable definitions or hidden-card fields were changed. Legendary styling is covered with a presentation fixture.

The director accepts only adjacent versions with matching game/event IDs, tracks the latest 128 accepted IDs and skips effects over 1.2 seconds late using a local elapsed-time estimate anchored to server time. Restores do not enqueue effects. A new cue replaces the current cue; there is no backlog. GSAP effects are cancelled at 700 ms and on unmount/motion changes. Damage/energy text always comes from the snapshot.

All particles are pointer-inert, clipped inside the space between unit rows and hidden from assistive technology. Essential controls and the countdown stay in HTML. Hidden tabs pause the canvas; reduced motion removes ambience, tilt, flips and particles. Audio is optional, quiet, synthesized locally, and muted by default.
